# Enterprise Usage Patterns

## Overview

The Governance Systems Skills Library can support enterprise governance workflows by standardizing how teams review, document, and communicate control-related decisions.

## Usage Pattern 1: Change Governance Review

Use the change governance and CAB readiness skills to review production changes before CAB.

Outputs may support:

- Jira internal notes.
- CAB agenda preparation.
- Conditional approval decisions.
- Evidence requirements.
- Risk escalation.

## Usage Pattern 2: Audit Evidence Review

Use audit evidence and control evidence skills before submitting evidence to auditors.

Outputs may support:

- Evidence completeness review.
- Owner questions.
- Submission cover notes.
- Traceability checks.
- Exception disclosure.

## Usage Pattern 3: Risk and RCA Governance

Use RCA and risk register skills to convert incidents and findings into structured governance records.

Outputs may support:

- RCA rewrite requests.
- Corrective action tracking.
- Risk register updates.
- Leadership summaries.
- Audit trail development.

## Usage Pattern 4: Third-Party and AI Governance

Use vendor risk and AI intake skills to evaluate new tools before approval.

Outputs may support:

- Vendor due diligence.
- DPA and SOC 2 tracking.
- AI terms review.
- Data flow questions.
- Non-production pilot guardrails.

## Usage Pattern 5: Executive Reporting

Use executive summary and governance metrics skills to turn operational governance data into leadership-ready reporting.

Outputs may support:

- Weekly summaries.
- Executive dashboards.
- Risk posture updates.
- Remediation tracking.
- Audit readiness status.
