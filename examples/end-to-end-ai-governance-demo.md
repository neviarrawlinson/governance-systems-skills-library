# End-to-End Demo: AI Governance Intake Workflow

## Scenario

A business team requests approval to pilot a third-party AI assistant that summarizes reporting trends and drafts leadership updates using internal reporting data.

## Workflow

### Step 1: AI Governance Intake Review

Skill:

`ai-governance-intake-review`

Output:

- AI governance decision
- Security, privacy, and data risks
- Prompt retention and model training questions
- Human review requirements
- Transparency requirements
- Approval gates

### Step 2: Third-Party Risk Review

Skill:

`third-party-risk-review`

Output:

- Vendor due diligence gaps
- SOC 2 and DPA requirements
- API and CSV export concerns
- Pilot guardrails

### Step 3: Policy Exception Review

Skill:

`policy-exception-review`

Output:

- Exception decision
- Compensating controls
- Expiration date
- Risk acceptance

### Step 4: Executive Summary

Skill:

`executive-grc-summary`

Output:

- Leadership-ready summary
- Risk posture
- Business impact
- Decision needed

## Value

This workflow demonstrates how AI governance should account for security, privacy, legal, data handling, transparency, human review, and operational risk before approval.
