## Purpose

The mappings help explain how practical GRC workflows connect to recognized control and governance themes.

They are useful for:

- Portfolio demonstration.
- Interview discussion.
- GRC training.
- Audit-readiness planning.
- Governance program design.
- Explaining the value of workflow-based GRC engineering.

## Important Note

These mappings are educational and practical. They are not legal advice, audit advice, regulatory interpretation, certification evidence, or a substitute for an organization's formal control mapping.
