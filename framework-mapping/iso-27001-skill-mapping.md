# ISO 27001 Skill Mapping

This page maps the Governance Systems Skills Library to ISO 27001-aligned governance themes. The mapping is intentionally theme-based because organizations must validate exact control applicability based on their own Statement of Applicability, scope, policies, and audit criteria.

## ISO 27001 Alignment Summary

| Skill | ISO 27001-Aligned Theme | Governance Value |
|---|---|---|
| `change-governance-review` | Change management, operational control, secure change practices | Helps confirm production changes include review, approval, validation, rollback, monitoring, and audit-ready evidence. |
| `cab-readiness-check` | Authorization, segregation of duties, change governance | Supports CAB decision quality by identifying blockers before implementation. |
| `rca-governance-analysis` | Incident learning, corrective action, continual improvement | Strengthens RCA quality by reviewing root cause depth, ownership, timelines, corrective actions, and monitoring gaps. |
| `audit-evidence-request` | Documented information and evidence readiness | Converts audit requests into evidence checklists and owner questions. |
| `risk-register-builder` | Risk identification, risk treatment, ownership | Converts findings and incidents into structured risk register entries. |
| `executive-grc-summary` | Governance accountability and management reporting | Provides leadership with concise status, impact, and action summaries. |
| `policy-exception-review` | Exception handling, risk acceptance, compensating controls | Reviews whether exceptions include rationale, approval, duration, ownership, and evidence. |
| `third-party-risk-review` | Supplier relationship management and data protection | Reviews supplier evidence, data sharing, integrations, security documentation, and approval readiness. |
| `control-evidence-quality-check` | Evidence sufficiency and control operation | Reviews whether evidence demonstrates control operation and traceability. |
| `governance-metrics-summary` | Performance evaluation and management review | Summarizes control gaps, trends, risks, exceptions, and remediation progress. |
| `ai-governance-intake-review` | New technology risk, supplier risk, data protection | Reviews AI tool usage, data handling, vendor evidence, prompt retention, transparency, and approval readiness. |

## Workflow-Based Mapping

### Change Governance

Relevant skills:

- `change-governance-review`
- `cab-readiness-check`
- `audit-evidence-request`
- `control-evidence-quality-check`

These skills support the operational side of change governance by checking whether technology changes are documented, reviewed, approved, tested, validated, monitored, and retained with sufficient evidence.

### Incident and RCA Governance

Relevant skills:

- `rca-governance-analysis`
- `risk-register-builder`
- `executive-grc-summary`

These skills support post-incident learning and governance accountability by linking incident records to root cause analysis, corrective actions, risk register updates, and leadership visibility.

### Supplier and Third-Party Governance

Relevant skills:

- `third-party-risk-review`
- `ai-governance-intake-review`
- `policy-exception-review`

These skills support supplier and third-party review by checking security evidence, privacy documentation, integration risk, data sharing, approvals, exception handling, and ownership.

### Evidence and Audit Readiness

Relevant skills:

- `audit-evidence-request`
- `control-evidence-quality-check`
- `governance-metrics-summary`

These skills support audit readiness by helping practitioners prepare evidence, identify gaps, explain exceptions, and summarize governance performance.
