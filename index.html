Governance Systems Skills Library

Claude-compatible skill packs for Governance Systems Engineering, change governance, RCA, audit evidence, risk documentation, vendor risk, AI governance intake, and executive GRC reporting.

View on GitHub

Purpose

This project helps GRC and IT governance practitioners turn governance expectations into repeatable workflows, decision logic, checklists, and audit-ready outputs.

Skill Packs

Change Governance

Change Governance Review

Reviews change requests for governance completeness, CAB readiness, risk, rollback, validation, monitoring, approvals, and audit readiness.

Change Governance

Cab Readiness Check

Determines whether a change request is ready for CAB review and decision-making.

Incident Governance

Rca Governance Analysis

Reviews RCA documentation for root cause quality, process gaps, monitoring gaps, ownership, corrective actions, and executive readiness.

Audit Readiness

Audit Evidence Request

Converts audit evidence requests into evidence checklists, owner questions, response plans, and submission guidance.

Risk Management

Risk Register Builder

Converts findings, issues, incidents, and observations into structured risk register entries.

Executive Reporting

Executive Grc Summary

Creates concise executive-ready summaries for governance, risk, compliance, audit, change, RCA, and control updates.

Policy Governance

Policy Exception Review

Reviews policy exception requests for business justification, risk impact, compensating controls, approval requirements, expiration date, ownership, and audit-ready documentation. Use when evaluating exceptions to security, IT, compliance, access, change management, vendor, or data governance policies.

Vendor Risk

Third Party Risk Review

Reviews vendors, SaaS tools, service providers, integrations, and third-party relationships for security, privacy, compliance, operational, data, and business risk. Use when assessing new vendors, renewals, questionnaires, SOC reports, DPAs, contracts, or application intake requests.

Audit Readiness

Control Evidence Quality Check

Evaluates audit evidence for completeness, accuracy, traceability, date coverage, reviewer signoff, control relevance, and submission readiness. Use when reviewing SOC 2, ISO 27001, ITGC, access review, change management, incident, vendor, or policy evidence before submitting to auditors.

Governance Reporting

Governance Metrics Summary

Turns operational governance data into leadership-ready metrics, trends, risks, exceptions, and action-oriented summaries. Use when summarizing CAB metrics, change volume, emergency changes, delayed closures, RCA actions, audit requests, evidence status, policy exceptions, or GRC program health.

AI Governance

Ai Governance Intake Review

Reviews proposed AI tools, models, automations, copilots, data uses, and AI-assisted workflows for governance, risk, privacy, security, compliance, ownership, transparency, and approval readiness. Use when evaluating AI tool intake, AI vendor requests, internal AI workflow proposals, or AI risk reviews.

Quality Controls

The library includes validation scripts, skill quality checklists, release readiness guidance, packaged skill files, and maintainer documentation.
