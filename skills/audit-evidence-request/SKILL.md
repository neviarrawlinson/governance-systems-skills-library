# Audit Evidence Request

## Purpose

Use this skill to help a GRC practitioner interpret an audit evidence request and turn it into a clear, organized evidence collection plan.

The skill should clarify what the auditor is asking for, what evidence would likely satisfy the request, who may own the evidence, and what quality checks should be performed before submission.

## Review Categories

1. Request Interpretation
   - What control, process, system, or period is being tested?
   - Is the request asking for population, sample evidence, policy evidence, approval evidence, operating evidence, or management review?
   - Is the request clear or ambiguous?

2. Evidence Needed
   - What artifacts are needed?
   - Are screenshots required?
   - Are exports, reports, approvals, logs, tickets, policies, or review records needed?
   - Does evidence need date and time visibility?

3. Evidence Owner
   - Which team or role likely owns the evidence?
   - Are multiple owners needed?
   - What follow-up questions should be sent to the owner?

4. Evidence Quality
   - Does the evidence show who performed the control?
   - Does it show when the control was performed?
   - Does it show what was reviewed or approved?
   - Does it show the result or outcome?
   - Does it cover the requested audit period?

5. Submission Readiness
   - Is the evidence complete?
   - Is it labeled clearly?
   - Are sensitive details minimized or handled appropriately?
   - Are explanations included where screenshots or exports need context?
